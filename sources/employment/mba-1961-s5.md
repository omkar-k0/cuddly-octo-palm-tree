A woman is entitled to maternity benefit, paid at the rate of her average
daily wage, for the period of her actual absence around delivery, provided
she has worked for the employer for at least eighty days in the twelve
months immediately before her expected date of delivery.

The maximum period of maternity benefit is twenty-six weeks, of which not
more than eight weeks may precede the expected date of delivery. For a woman
who already has two or more surviving children, the maximum period is twelve
weeks.
