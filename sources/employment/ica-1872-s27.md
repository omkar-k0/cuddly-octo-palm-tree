Every agreement by which anyone is restrained from exercising a lawful
profession, trade or business of any kind is to that extent void. The only
exception in the section concerns a person who sells the goodwill of a
business, who may agree not to carry on a similar business within specified
local limits.

Courts have generally treated restrictions that stop an employee from
working for a competitor after the employment has ended as void under this
section, while restrictions that apply during the period of employment, and
obligations to protect confidential information, are treated differently.
