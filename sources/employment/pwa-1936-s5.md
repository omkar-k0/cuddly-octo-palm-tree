Wages must be paid within fixed time limits after the end of each wage
period, and no wage period may exceed one month. In smaller establishments
wages are due before the expiry of the seventh day after the last day of the
wage period; in larger establishments, before the tenth day.

Where the employment of a person is terminated by or on behalf of the
employer, the wages already earned must be paid before the expiry of the
second working day from the day the employment is terminated.

Whether the Act applies to a particular employee can depend on their wage
level and on the type of establishment.
