An employer with ten or more employees must constitute an Internal
Committee to receive complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace
(Section 4). Where there is no Internal Committee, or the complaint is
against the employer, a complaint can be made to the Local Committee for the
district.

An aggrieved woman may make a complaint in writing to the Internal
Committee, or the Local Committee if there is no Internal Committee, within
three months from the date of the incident, or of the last incident in a
series. The Committee may extend this by a further three months if it is
satisfied that circumstances prevented the complaint being made in time
(Section 9).

Making a complaint under this Act does not prevent the woman from also
making a complaint to the police where the conduct is an offence.
