The Industrial Relations Code, 2020 replaces the Industrial Disputes Act,
1947. It keeps the core conditions before a worker with at least one year of
continuous service can be retrenched: written notice with reasons (or wages
in lieu of notice), retrenchment compensation at the rate of fifteen days'
average pay for every completed year of continuous service, and notice to
the appropriate Government.

The Code also provides for a worker re-skilling fund, to which an employer
contributes an amount linked to the retrenched worker's wages, to be
credited to the worker.

Larger establishments need prior permission of the Government before
lay-off, retrenchment or closure; the employee threshold for this is set in
the Code.
