The Code on Social Security, 2020 brings the law on gratuity into a single
code alongside provident fund, maternity benefit and other social security
schemes. It continues the basic scheme of gratuity on the end of
employment after a qualifying period of continuous service, calculated on
the basis of fifteen days' wages for each completed year of service.

The Code contains special provisions for fixed-term employees and certain
other categories of employees, under which gratuity may be payable without
completing five years of continuous service. Whether these provisions apply
depends on the type of employment and on the date from which the Code is in
force.
