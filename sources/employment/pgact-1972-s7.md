A person eligible for gratuity applies to the employer in the prescribed
form. The employer determines the amount, gives notice of it, and arranges
payment within thirty days from the date gratuity becomes payable. If the
employer does not pay within that period, the employer is liable to pay
simple interest at a rate notified by the Government, unless the delay was
due to the employee's own fault.

If there is a dispute about the amount, about whether gratuity is payable,
or about who is entitled to it, the matter can be referred to the
Controlling Authority appointed under the Act, which can hold an inquiry and
decide the amount payable.
