A "workman" is any person employed in an industry to do manual, unskilled,
skilled, technical, operational, clerical or supervisory work for hire or
reward. It does not include a person employed mainly in a managerial or
administrative capacity, or a person employed in a supervisory capacity who
draws wages above the limit stated in the Act.

Whether someone is a workman depends on the main nature of the work they
actually do, not on their job title. Many protections against retrenchment
and unfair termination apply only to workmen; employees outside the
definition rely mainly on their contract of employment.
